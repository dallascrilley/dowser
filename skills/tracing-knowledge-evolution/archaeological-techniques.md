# Archaeological Techniques

## The Stratigraphic Dig

Start with current knowledge and dig down through temporal layers, dating each conceptual stratum and mapping the geological column of ideas. Identify discontinuities and catastrophes in knowledge evolution.

**Process:**
1. Begin with current state of knowledge
2. Dig down layer by layer
3. Date each conceptual stratum
4. Map the geological column of ideas
5. Identify discontinuities (sudden breaks)
6. Recognize catastrophes (mass obsolescence events)

## The Artifact Analysis

Examine conceptual artifacts to determine their age, origin, and original use. Assess current relevance and decide on preservation priority.

**Analysis Steps:**
1. Identify the artifact (concept, pattern, practice)
2. Determine age (when it emerged)
3. Trace origin (where it came from)
4. Document original use (problem it solved)
5. Assess current relevance
6. Decide preservation priority

## The Genealogical Trace

Trace modern concepts back through their ancestors, identifying branching points and mapping family trees. Find lost cousins and extinct branches.

**Tracing Process:**
1. Start with modern concept
2. Identify direct ancestors
3. Map branching points
4. Create family tree
5. Find lost cousins (abandoned alternatives)
6. Document extinct branches

## The Fossil Hunt

Look for traces of dead ideas, examining why they died and assessing preservation quality. Consider revival potential and document findings in the fossil record.

**Hunting Process:**
1. Search for traces of dead ideas
2. Examine death causes
3. Assess preservation quality
4. Evaluate revival potential
5. Document in fossil record
6. Flag for future excavation if promising

## The Time Capsule Creation

Preserve ideas that might be needed later, including context for future understanding. Document preservation reasons and create retrieval instructions.

**Preservation Process:**
1. Identify ideas with future potential
2. Capture complete context
3. Document preservation rationale
4. Create retrieval instructions
5. Tag with relevance conditions
6. Store in accessible format
